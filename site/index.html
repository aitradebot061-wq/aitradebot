Three things no other trading bot gives you

All three live inside the ATB token contract on BNB Chain. Anyone can read them. Nobody can fake them.

01 · PROOF

A track record you can't photoshop

Each time the bot fills an order, it anchors a hash of the trade record on-chain, including the losing ones. Backtests can be tuned; a timestamped chain of hashes can't.

anchorTrade(agent, logHash) · reputation(agent)
02 · GUARD

A blast radius, not a blank check

Registered bot wallets get a 24-hour spend cap and an operator kill switch. If the bot is hijacked or misfires, you lose at most one day's cap, then it's frozen.

setDailyLimit(agent, limit) · freezeAgent(agent)
03 · BOND

Skin in the game, on-chain

Whoever runs a bot locks an ATB bond. Misbehave and an arbiter slashes it, burned forever, with the reason written to the chain. Report a vulnerability and get paid from escrowed bounties.

registerAgent(...) · slash(agent, amount, reason) · postBounty(...)
